ERGONOMOUSE SETUP 4.1
=====================

1. Windows: run the installer. Linux: open the AppImage. macOS: drag the app
   from the DMG to Applications. See UNSIGNED_INSTALL.txt if your system asks
   you to confirm the unsigned application.
2. Open ErgonoMouse Setup. Your browser will show a private page from this
   computer.
3. Connect the Arduino Pro Micro with a USB data cable.
4. Follow the six short screens; the app identifies and configures your model.
5. Install the verified firmware, then follow each guided motion check.

Nothing is uploaded and no account is required.

If the app does not open a browser, visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
